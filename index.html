Your Guide

Nolan Souza

Nolan grew up on the Kitsap Peninsula with a bike under him and trails on the brain, and now calls Bellevue, Washington home. Duthie Hill, Tiger Mountain, Raging River — he's spent more time in the PNW forests than out of them, and that obsession is exactly what makes riding with him so good. He lives and breathes this stuff, and it shows the moment you hit the trail together.

He started this company because he loves sharing the trails just as much as riding them. Nolan has a rare ability to meet riders exactly where they are — calm with beginners, fired up with riders who want to push — and he'll find the line that makes you stop at the bottom and say "let's do that again."

Your Guide

Jake Martin

Jake lives on the Kitsap Peninsula and gravitates toward the steep and committing: punishing climbs, janky technical descents, the kind of lines that demand your full attention. Port Gamble is basically his backyard, and he knows every root, rock, and jump in the mountain bike park.

Beyond his riding, Jake is an exceptional mechanic. Whether it's a mid-ride mechanical or a full build, he knows bikes inside and out — so when something goes sideways on the trail, you're in the best hands possible. On a tour, he brings that same intensity and expertise to the group: direct, encouraging, and genuinely pumped to help riders push past what they thought they could do. If you want to be challenged — technically, physically, or both — Jake's your guy.

Trail Systems We Ride

Raging River State Forest

Old-growth ferns, ancient cedar groves, and surprisingly technical terrain just 30 minutes from downtown Seattle. First time riders and seasoned shredders alike will find something to love.

Explore trails ›
Tiger Mountain

Washington's most popular trail system, right in Seattle's backyard. Over 100 miles of trail ranging from beginner-friendly flow to technical old-growth singletrack. The West Tiger 3 descent is a rite of passage.

Explore trails ›
Duthie Hill

King County's premier purpose-built bike park. Berms, jumps, drops, and wooden features designed for progression. Perfect for building skills and having a blast doing it.

Explore trails ›
Galbraith Mountain

Bellingham's crown jewel. Over 65 miles of machine-built trails. The Chuckanut Range backdrop and consistently maintained berms make this a bucket-list stop.

Explore trails ›
Port Gamble Mountain Bike Park

Kitsap Peninsula's best-kept secret. Over 30 miles of purpose-built flow trails winding through mossy second-growth forest, with Hood Canal views thrown in for free. A ferry ride never felt so worth it.
